🌟 Today's Topic

Today I started learning the fundamentals of Natural Language Processing (NLP).

NLP is an important area of Artificial Intelligence that helps computers understand, process, and work with human language.

Examples include chatbots, voice assistants, translation systems, sentiment analysis, and search engines.

---

🧠 What is NLP?

NLP stands for Natural Language Processing.

It is a branch of AI that allows computers to process and understand human language in the form of:

- 📝 Text
- 🗣️ Speech
- 💬 Conversations
- 📄 Documents

Simple Example

Human language:

«""I love learning Artificial Intelligence.""»

A computer cannot directly understand this sentence like a human.

NLP helps convert the text into a form that a machine-learning model can process.

---

🔍 Important NLP Terms

1️⃣ Corpus

A corpus is a collection of text documents used for NLP tasks.

Example:

Document 1 → I love AI.
Document 2 → Machine learning is interesting.
Document 3 → NLP helps computers understand language.

Together, these documents form a corpus.

---

2️⃣ Document

A single piece of text is called a document.

Example:

"I am learning Python."

---

3️⃣ Token

A token is a smaller unit of text.

For example:

"I love AI"

can become:

["I", "love", "AI"]

Each word is a token.

---

4️⃣ Tokenization

Tokenization means splitting text into smaller units called tokens.

Example:

Input:
I love Machine Learning

Output:
I
love
Machine
Learning

---

5️⃣ Vocabulary

The collection of unique words in a text dataset is called the vocabulary.

Example:

"I love AI. I love Python."

Vocabulary:
I
love
AI
Python

---

6️⃣ Stop Words

Stop words are commonly occurring words that may not provide much useful information for some NLP tasks.

Examples:

the
is
a
an
and
of
in

Depending on the task, these words may be removed during preprocessing.

---

🧹 Basic NLP Pipeline

A simple NLP workflow can be represented as:

Raw Text
   ↓
Text Cleaning
   ↓
Tokenization
   ↓
Remove Unwanted Words
   ↓
Feature Extraction
   ↓
Machine Learning Model
   ↓
Prediction / Result

---

📊 Word Frequency

Word frequency means counting how many times each word appears in a text.

Example:

AI is powerful.
AI is useful.
AI is interesting.

Frequency:

AI → 3
is → 3
powerful → 1
useful → 1
interesting → 1

Word frequency can help us understand which words occur most often in a dataset.

---

🧮 Text to Numbers

Machine-learning models work with numerical data.

Therefore, text needs to be converted into numbers.

One basic technique is Bag of Words (BoW).

Example:

"I love AI"
"I love Python"

Vocabulary:

AI
I
love
Python

The sentences can then be represented using numbers based on how many times each word appears.

This creates a Document-Term Matrix.
